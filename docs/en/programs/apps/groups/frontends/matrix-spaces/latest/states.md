# Matrix Spaces - States

> [Matrix Spaces](../index.md)

## Access states

- Room restricted to "Space members": a person outside the space cannot join without an invitation [S2].
- Joining without invitation in an encrypted room: earlier history is not shown [S2].
- Private (invite only) room: only explicitly invited people can join [S2].
