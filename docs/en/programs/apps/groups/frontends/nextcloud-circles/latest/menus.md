# Nextcloud Circles - Menus

> [Nextcloud Circles](../index.md)

## Menu inventory

| Place | Item | Effect | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left menu | "+" beside Teams | Starts creating a team (name entry) | S1 |
| Team configuration screen | Add members | Adds local accounts, groups, email addresses, other teams | S1 |
| Member row | Three-dot menu next to a user | Changes the member's role | S1 |
| Teams app | Teams management | Teams are managed in the separate Teams app | S1 |
