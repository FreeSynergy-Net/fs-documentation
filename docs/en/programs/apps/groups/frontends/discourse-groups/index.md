# Discourse Groups

> Category: [groups](../../index.md)

## What it is
Discourse groups are member lists inside the Discourse forum software. Admins appoint owners per group, and owners manage that group's membership [S1]. Visibility and access settings decide who can see a group, join it, or ask to join [S2].

## Notable ideas
A membership request goes to the group owners, who get it with accept and reject links [S2].
