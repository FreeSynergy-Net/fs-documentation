# Mailman 3 - Features

> [Mailman 3](../index.md)

## Personal list settings
- Delivery status: mail on or off per list. Turning it off does not re-enable itself [S1].
- Delivery mode: single posts, MIME digests or plain text digests [S1].
- Receive own postings: choose whether to get a copy of your own posts; no effect on digests [S1].
- Acknowledge posts: get a confirmation when your post goes through [S1].
- Hide address: keeps your address off the public member list; it is not hidden in archives or outgoing mail [S1].
- Avoid duplicates: skip copies when your address is already in To or Cc [S1].

## Scope of settings
- Settings apply globally, per address, or per list. Per-list overrides per-address, which overrides global [S1].

## Account
- Sign up is optional before subscribing [S1].
- Subscribing without an account is possible; an account under the same address is needed later to change settings [S1].
