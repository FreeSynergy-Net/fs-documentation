# Google Groups - Features

> [Google Groups](../index.md) · Features

## Membership

- Create a group with name, optional description, email address and settings [S2].
- Add members directly (they join at once) or invite them (they must accept) [S3].
- Pending invitations can be resent or cancelled [S3].
- Assign member, manager or owner role when adding directly [S3].
- Leave a group, or switch email delivery off per group [S5].

## Posting and viewing

- Who can post, who can view conversations, who can view the member list [S2].
- Custom roles are available for posting; member viewing and member management cannot use custom roles [S6].
- Restrict posting without deleting the group [S4].

## Moderation

- Message moderation: none, moderate non-member messages, or moderate all messages [S2].
- New member restrictions: none, moderate new members, or block new members from posting [S2].
- Spam handling: reject, or send for review with or without notifying moderators [S2].
- Who may approve, block and delete messages is set separately [S2].

## Workspace-only

- Auto replies, the option to post suspicious messages, and Collaborative Inbox (needs Google Groups for Business) [S2].
