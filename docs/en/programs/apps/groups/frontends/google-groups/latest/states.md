# Google Groups - States

> [Google Groups](../index.md) · States

## Group states

- Group created, not ready: wait a few minutes before sending messages [S2].
- Deleted: cannot be restored; removed from search and the directory [S4].
- Posting restricted by owner or manager (group still exists) [S4].

## Member and invitation states

- Invitation pending: invitee must accept; pending list shows resend/cancel [S3].
- Email delivery off per member (subscription "no email") [S5].
- Member is required to stay (admin-required group in work or school accounts): cannot leave [S5].

## Access states

- Option not shown: work/school-only or admin-controlled option [S2].
- Owner rights limited by admin, but not the right to view members or conversations [S2].
- Child account cannot be added [S3].
- External user cannot be invited to an internal group until the group is changed to internal [S3].
- Member cannot be added by a manager without permission (linked to blocking help) [S5].
